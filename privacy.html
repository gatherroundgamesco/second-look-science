Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

SLS Uploader is an internal tool used only by the Second Look Science channel to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

The tool accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account, to upload videos and read basic channel information. It does not collect, sell, or share any data about viewers or other users. Access tokens are stored privately and used only for uploads, and access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.

Use of YouTube is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
